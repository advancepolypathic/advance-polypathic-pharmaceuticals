PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT

DOXYCHEM

Doxycycline for Injection USP 100mg

DOXYCHEM is a prescription antibiotic injection containing Doxycycline 100 mg. It is indicated for the treatment of susceptible bacterial infections and is administered by intravenous infusion under the direction of a qualified healthcare professional.

Composition

Each vial contains:

Ingredient Strength
Doxycycline for Injection USP Equivalent to Doxycycline 100 mg

Presentation: Doxycycline Injection 100 mg

Route: For I.V. infusion only

Benefits

  • Doxycycline is a tetracycline-class antibiotic with activity against susceptible bacteria.
  • It helps inhibit bacterial protein synthesis and bacterial growth.
  • It may be used when an injectable form of doxycycline is clinically appropriate.
  • It can be used for susceptible bacterial infections according to the physician's assessment.
  • Appropriate antibiotic selection should be based on the suspected or confirmed infection and medical evaluation.

Uses

  • Treatment of susceptible bacterial infections.
  • Certain respiratory tract infections.
  • Certain urinary and genital infections when the causative organism is susceptible.
  • Certain skin and soft-tissue infections.
  • Other infections for which doxycycline therapy is clinically indicated.

Possible Side Effects

  • Nausea
  • Vomiting
  • Diarrhea
  • Headache
  • Abdominal discomfort
  • Photosensitivity or increased sensitivity to sunlight
  • Injection-site or infusion-related reactions
  • Rarely, allergic reactions or other serious reactions
Side effects may vary from person to person. Seek immediate medical attention if severe allergic reactions, breathing difficulty or other serious symptoms occur.

Precautions

  • Use only on the prescription and supervision of a qualified physician.
  • This injection should be administered by qualified healthcare personnel.
  • Inform the doctor about any allergy to doxycycline or other tetracycline antibiotics.
  • Inform the doctor about liver problems or other medical conditions.
  • Use caution with prolonged treatment and follow the physician's instructions.
  • Protect yourself from excessive sunlight during treatment.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

Directions for Use

  • Use only as directed by the physician.
  • DOXYCHEM is intended for intravenous infusion as directed by the product information.
  • It should be prepared and administered by qualified healthcare personnel.
  • The dose and duration depend on the type and severity of infection and the patient's condition.
  • Complete the prescribed course unless the treating physician advises otherwise.

Storage

  • Store according to the manufacturer's recommended storage conditions.
  • Protect the product from excessive heat, light and moisture.
  • Keep the vial in its original packaging until use.
  • Do not use the product after the expiry date.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
