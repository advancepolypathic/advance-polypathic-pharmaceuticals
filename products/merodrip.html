PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT

MERODRIP

Meropenem Injection IP 1000 mg

MERODRIP is a prescription antibiotic injection containing Meropenem 1000 mg. Meropenem belongs to the carbapenem class of antibiotics and is used for the treatment of serious bacterial infections when injectable antibiotic therapy is required. It is intended for I.V. use only under the direction and supervision of a qualified healthcare professional.

Composition

Each vial contains:

Ingredient Strength
Meropenem Sterile IP Equivalent to Meropenem 1000 mg

Presentation: Injection 1 gm

Route: For I.V. use only

Benefits / Therapeutic Role

  • Meropenem is a broad-spectrum carbapenem antibiotic.
  • It works by interfering with bacterial cell-wall formation.
  • It is used for serious bacterial infections requiring injectable antibiotic therapy.
  • The choice of antibiotic and duration of treatment should be determined by the treating physician.

Uses

  • Severe bacterial infections
  • Respiratory tract infections
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Intra-abdominal infections
  • Skin and skin-structure infections
  • Serious infections when prescribed by a physician

Possible Side Effects

  • Diarrhea
  • Nausea
  • Vomiting
  • Headache
  • Rash
  • Itching
  • Injection-site reactions
Side effects may vary between individuals. Seek medical attention if severe allergic reactions, persistent diarrhea or other unusual symptoms occur.

Precautions

  • Use only on the prescription and supervision of a qualified physician.
  • This is a prescription injection and should not be self-administered.
  • Inform the doctor about any previous allergy to meropenem or other beta-lactam antibiotics.
  • Tell the doctor about all medicines being taken.
  • Inform the doctor about any history of seizures or neurological disorders.
  • Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding only after medical advice.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

Directions for Use

  • Use only as directed by the physician.
  • For I.V. use only.
  • Reconstitute and administer according to the approved product instructions.
  • The injection should be prepared and administered by qualified healthcare personnel.
  • Do not self-administer this medicine.

Storage

  • Store as directed on the product label.
  • Protect the product from excessive heat, light and moisture.
  • Keep the vial in its original packaging until use.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
