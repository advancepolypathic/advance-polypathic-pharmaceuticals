PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT

PULMOCORT-HS

Hydrocortisone Sodium Succinate Injection IP

PULMOCORT-HS is a prescription corticosteroid injection containing Hydrocortisone Sodium Succinate equivalent to Hydrocortisone 100 mg. It is intended for I.M./I.V. use under the direction of a qualified physician.

Composition

Each vial contains:

Ingredient Strength
Hydrocortisone Sodium Succinate (Sterile) IP Equivalent to Hydrocortisone 100 mg

Presentation: Injection 100 mg

Route: For I.M./I.V. use only

Benefits / Therapeutic Role

  • Hydrocortisone is a corticosteroid used for its anti-inflammatory and glucocorticoid effects.
  • It may be used when systemic corticosteroid therapy is required.
  • It can be used under medical supervision in conditions requiring hydrocortisone replacement or corticosteroid treatment.
  • Its use and dose depend on the patient's medical condition and physician's assessment.

Uses

  • Used for conditions where systemic corticosteroid treatment is indicated.
  • May be used in certain allergic and inflammatory conditions.
  • May be used for corticosteroid replacement in appropriate clinical situations.
  • May be used in emergency medical conditions when prescribed by a physician.

Possible Side Effects

  • Increased blood sugar
  • Increased blood pressure
  • Fluid retention
  • Stomach discomfort
  • Mood changes
  • Difficulty sleeping
  • Increased risk of infection with prolonged or high-dose use
Side effects depend on the dose, duration and individual patient. Seek medical attention if severe or unusual symptoms occur.

Precautions

  • Use only on the prescription and supervision of a qualified physician.
  • This is a prescription injection and should not be self-administered.
  • Inform the doctor about diabetes, high blood pressure, infections or other medical conditions.
  • Tell the doctor about all medicines being taken.
  • Long-term corticosteroid therapy should not generally be stopped suddenly without medical advice.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

Directions for Use

  • Use only as directed by the physician.
  • Reconstitute with the supplied sterile water for injection as directed.
  • The reconstituted solution should be used immediately according to the product instructions.
  • For I.M./I.V. use only.
  • Administration should be performed by qualified healthcare personnel.

Storage

  • Protect from moisture.
  • Store at a temperature not exceeding 30°C.
  • Store in the original packaging.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
